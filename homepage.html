Isange One Stop Centre

Providing integrated services under one roof

Our Mission

Rwanda's Unified Response to Gender-Based Violence

The Isange One Stop Centre (IOSC) model — pioneered by the Rwanda National Police — brings together medical, psychosocial, legal, and law enforcement support for GBV survivors under a single roof. No survivor needs to travel between multiple institutions to access justice and care.

The GBV Management Information System digitises every step of this process: from the moment a case is reported, through investigation, referral and closure — maintaining confidentiality, accountability, and oversight at every stage.

Strictly Confidential

All survivor data encrypted and access-controlled by role

Rights-Based Approach

Survivor dignity and safety at the centre of every decision

Police, hospitals, courts and social services connected

System Features

Everything Needed to Fight GBV — In One Platform

Case Registration

Register GBV cases digitally with full survivor and suspect profiles, incident details, location data, and priority classification.

Investigation Tracking

Log every investigation action, track assigned officers, set next-action dates, and maintain a full audit trail of case progress.

Smart Notifications

Commanders receive instant alerts for new cases, officer updates and stale cases. Officers are notified the moment they are assigned a case.

Evidence Management

Securely upload and store evidence files — photos, documents, audio, medical reports — linked directly to cases with tamper-evident records.

Referral Tracking

Track referrals to hospitals, courts, shelters, and social services. Monitor completion status and follow-up actions for each referral.

Reports & Analytics

Generate summary reports by type, province, district and month. Export to CSV. Charts and dashboards give commanders instant situational awareness.

How It Works

From Report to Resolution — Every Step Tracked

1

Survivor Arrives / Case Reported

A GBV case is reported — in person, by phone, or by a third party. A Data Entry officer registers the case digitally into the system immediately.

2

Commander Assigns an Officer

The Admin or Commander receives an instant notification, reviews the case, sets priority and assigns a qualified investigating officer.

3

Investigation & Evidence Collection

The assigned officer logs all actions — interviews, evidence collected, referrals made — directly in the system. Every update notifies command.

4

Resolution & Reporting

Cases are closed, referred or escalated with full documentation. Reports are generated for national statistics, oversight and policy-making.

Awareness & Education

There Is No Excuse for Gender-Based Violence

Join Rwanda's Commitment to Zero Tolerance on GBV

The Rwanda National Police is committed to a Rwanda where every person — regardless of gender, age or background — lives free from violence and fear.

Sexual Violence

Recognising Sexual Violence

Sexual violence — including rape, sexual assault, harassment and exploitation — is a crime under Rwandan law. Survivors deserve immediate medical care, legal protection and psychosocial support.
